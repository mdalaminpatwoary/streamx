Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Terms

StreamX (“we”, “us”) sells digital subscriptions to customers in Bangladesh through this website. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, mobile number, email address and what you bought, so we can deliver and support your order.
  • Subscription records: the service, plan, start and expiry dates, and the login details for your subscription (for example the account email, profile or PIN), so you can see, use and renew your subscriptions.
  • Sign-in: if you sign in with Google or Microsoft, we receive only your verified email address from them. We do not receive your password, contacts or other data. Email sign-in links are sent to the address you enter.

How we use it

  • To process and deliver your order and show your subscriptions in My account.
  • To contact you about your order, renewals and support, by email, SMS or WhatsApp.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the service secure.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

My account and the chat assistant

When you are signed in, My account and the chat assistant can show you your own subscriptions, days left, login details and orders. This information is shown only to you, in your browser. Passwords, PINs and codes stay hidden until you tap Show, and they are hidden again after a short time. They are never saved in the chat history. We never show your details to anyone who is not signed in.

The chat assistant answers from this website's own information. It does not send your questions to an outside AI service. If you choose to chat with a person, you do that on WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram, which have their own privacy policies.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use analytics tools that follow you across the web. To make the site work, your browser keeps a few small items on your own device:

  • Sign-in session: keeps you logged in to My account until you sign out.
  • Cart: remembers what you added to your cart.
  • Chat assistant: keeps your conversation on this device while you browse.
  • Short-lived settings: for example, whether you closed the chat bubble, and a quick copy of your account page (kept for under two minutes) so it opens faster.

When you sign in with Google or Microsoft, we set one temporary security cookie that lasts 10 minutes. It only confirms that the sign-in request came from you. Service pages may play trailers from YouTube, which we load in its privacy-enhanced mode, so it does not set cookies until you press play. You can clear this data at any time in your browser settings, or by signing out.

Payments

Online payments are handled by EPS (Easy Payment System) on its own secure page. We never see or store your card number, wallet PIN or password. We keep only the payment reference and status. When you renew from My account or the chat assistant, your plan is extended after payment is confirmed, and we do not charge you automatically.

Who we share it with

We use trusted providers to run the site: Supabase (database), Vercel (hosting), Resend (email delivery) and EPS (payments). They process data only to provide those services. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order and subscription records for as long as needed to support your purchases and meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by messaging us on WhatsApp (+880 1312-850241), on Messenger or Instagram (streamx.com.bd), or by email at streamx.com.bd@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
