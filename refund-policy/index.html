Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Digital subscriptions are delivered electronically, so they cannot be returned like physical goods. This policy explains when you are eligible for a refund, when a refund charge may apply, and how we fix problems with your subscription.

By placing an order online, you confirm that you have read and agree to this Refund Policy, our Terms & Conditions and our Privacy Policy.

1. Delivery time

Most subscriptions are delivered within 30 minutes of confirmed payment, and some can take up to 3 hours. We send your account details to your email and WhatsApp, and you can follow the status of every order in My account.

2. When you are eligible for a refund

You are eligible for a full refund if we cannot deliver your subscription within 24 hours of confirmed payment. Please message us on WhatsApp with your order details, and we will verify the order and process your refund to the payment method you used, or to another method we agree with you.

3. When a refund charge may apply

If your subscription was delivered, or if you cancel an order that is already being prepared, any refund is reviewed case by case and a refund charge may apply to cover the cost of activating the subscription and processing the payment. We will tell you the exact amount before anything is refunded, so you can decide whether to continue.

4. If your subscription has a problem

If an eligible subscription stops working or does not match your plan during its stated validity, message us on WhatsApp. We will fix it or replace it with a working one. A refund is considered only if we cannot fix or replace it.

5. How to ask for help or a refund

  1. Message us on WhatsApp as soon as you notice the problem or decide to cancel.
  2. Send your order details: the service and plan, the email or phone you used, and your Transaction ID from the payment page or EPS receipt.
  3. Describe the problem and, if you can, send a screenshot.
  4. We check and resolve it. We fix the issue, send a replacement, or approve your refund. Once a refund is approved, we tell you the amount and how it will be paid.

6. What is not covered

  • Problems caused by changing the account email, password or profile details, or by sharing access beyond what your plan allows (for example, using a single-device plan on several devices).
  • Subscriptions that were already used up or have passed their validity period.
  • Changes of mind after a subscription has been delivered and activated, except where a refund is approved after review (see section 3).
  • Problems that come from the service itself being unavailable in your region or being changed by the brand.

7. Before you buy

Please check the service, plan, validity and price before you pay. If you are not sure which plan is right for you, ask us on WhatsApp first. We are happy to help you choose.
